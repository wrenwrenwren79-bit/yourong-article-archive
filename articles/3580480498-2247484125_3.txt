General 部分 | C -- Customer  B -- Barista
C: Can I get a iced latte?
B: Yes, sure, to-go ？(比较流行说to-go， take away也可以）or for here？
C: Can I have a latte?
B: Yes, hot or iced?
C: Hot, please
B: 35 Yuan (RMB) , do you pay by cash, wechat or alipay?
C: Alipay please
B: Okay, I scan you
...假装扫码支付了
B: Take a seat, your coffee will be ready in a minute, I will bring it to your table.我们跟本国客人会习惯性地招呼一下说稍等，咖啡一会就好，可是碰到外国客人，很多时候都是把银家往旁边一晾就完事了
Extra talks:
B: Do you need sugar?
C: Yes, I think I need a little, Can I have some syrup?
B: We have cane sugar which is free of charge, if you want to add the flavor syrup, we have vanilla and carmamel , but with a 2 RMB extra charge.
